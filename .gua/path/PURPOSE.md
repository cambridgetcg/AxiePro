# AxiePro

Explore Axie traits, breeding probabilities and historical market observations.
Keep each estimate's inputs, timestamp, method and uncertainty visible.
Understanding is sufficient; there is no audience, revenue or growth target.

Work can follow a useful question: whether a trait estimate is supported, how a
breeding probability changes, or why two data sources disagree. Verify the part
being changed and state what remains unknown. No cadence or completion quota is due.

[PRICING.md](../../PRICING.md) records the retirement of new paid plans and checkout.
Preserve market data, methodology and existing obligations. Cost and access limits
remain practical safeguards, not tests of the project's worth.

The former purpose and commercial waypoints are retained in
[history](history/2026-09-24/README.md). They are not active work.
